The framework-prefix.jar fixture contains unmodified JVM classfiles selected from:

- org.scalatest:scalatest-core_2.13:3.2.19
- org.scalatest:scalatest-compatible:3.2.19
- org.scalactic:scalactic_2.13:3.2.19
- org.scala-lang:scala-library:2.13.18

ScalaTest identifies its vendor as Artima, Inc. The published Maven POMs declare
the Apache License, Version 2.0; that license is included in LICENSE-APACHE.
The exact original artifact and selected archive hashes are pinned in manifest.json.
Only the JAR container/class selection differs; classfile bytes are unmodified.

The prefix also contains java/io/Serializable.class from the exact OpenJDK 21
java.base module used for compilation and JVM validation. Its corresponding
Serializable.java source, GPLv2 with Classpath Exception license, additional license
information and assembly exception are preserved under third-party/openjdk/.
The original JMOD/source archive hashes are pinned separately in manifest.json.

The own source classes and bytecode.jar under base/ and head/ are generated from
the Scala source in this fixture. No hand-authored runtime graph is embedded.
